Meet the Baker

Welcome to Treats by Serine!

Hi! I’m Serine, the heart behind Treats by Serine. I’ve always dreamed of starting my own business, and with my love for sweet treats, baking and all things desserts, I decided to turn that passion into something special.

What started as a love for delicious sweets and the idea of creating my own little business has grown into a place where I can share that passion with others.

I believe every sweet treat should bring a little happiness. From freshly baked cookies to delicious desserts and custom treats, everything is made with love, care and attention to detail.

Thank you for supporting my little business and being part of my journey. I’m so excited to share all things sweet with you! ♡
